How PuzzsiC works

PuzzsiC is a free music puzzle game. Every tile is a 1.5-second slice of a song. Tap a tile to hear it, drag it into the grid, and the album cover comes into focus as the pieces land in order.

Pick a mystery disc, solve the puzzle and name the song. Cover photo and Music video puzzles feature OPM favorites. Random music shuffles global songs from the 80s, 90s, 2000s, 2010s and what is trending now, so you can play with music from anywhere in the world.

You can also make your own challenge: search any song, trim the clip, and send the link to a friend. Play as a guest, or sign in to save your times and challenges across devices.
